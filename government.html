Government of Satriale

The government of Satriale is a representative republic, where elected officials serve the people. The head of state is chosen through a democratic council, and decisions are made for the good of all.

Multiple branches exist to ensure balance: a legislative body that writes laws, an executive that enforces them, and a judiciary that interprets them. Transparency and civic involvement are fundamental.
