Government Structure

Doge: Head of State. Oversees national governance, diplomacy, and security.

Dons: Local rulers managing Holds. Form the Council to vote on State measures and elect Doges.

Sindacos: Mayors elected in cities to manage local affairs, organize events, and liaise with Dons.

State Offices: Appointed by the Doge or Dons. Citizens may be appointed based on loyalty and contributions.
