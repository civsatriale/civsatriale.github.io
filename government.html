The Doge

The Doge serves as Head of State and wields the highest authority in diplomacy, governance, and national security.

The Dons

Dons govern individual Holds and vote on national decisions through the Council of Dons.

The Citizenry

  • Compagno – Base citizenship; land rights; iron gear awarded.
  • Uomo D’Onore – High citizenship; factory access; diamond gear awarded.

Sindacos

Elected Mayors responsible for local leadership and communication between citizens and Dons.
