Article I – The State

The State shall be known as The Serene Republic of Satriale...

The State shall be known as The Serene Republic of Satriale. The land shall be divided between state-administered territory and Don-administered Holds. The State shall be established as a protectorate of The Sovereign Republic of Skandic. Independence from protectorateship may only be declared given a unanimous vote of the Council of Dons, and support of the Skandic executive (Prime Minister) and majority vote of its legislature (Senate) All territory within its recognized borders shall be sovereign and subject to this Constitution. Matters of the State and matters of Religion shall remain separate. The Papacy, or any other religious establishment, shall hold no official role in government, but shall nonetheless remain welcome in the Republic. All religions and their practitioners shall enjoy the freedom to worship, organize, and thrive within these borders, so long as the practice does not violate this Constitution nor any State law. The official flag of the Serene Republic of Satriale shall be the image attached as Figure 1. Article II - The Dons Each recognized Don shall govern a Hold consisting of their developed and administered lands. The Don holds authority over local laws, construction, and civil order within their Hold , so long as such laws do not conflict with this Constitution or State law. The Dons collectively form The Council of Dons (hereinafter called the Council), which shall advise the Doge, vote on State measures, and elect future Doges. Article III - The Doge The Doge shall serve as Head of State and ultimate authority in matters of diplomacy, state governance, and national security. The Doge may be removed from office by a two-thirds vote of the Council.. A new Doge may then be elected from among the Dons with a simple majority. The Doge shall retain their Donship and remain a voting member of the Council during their tenure. The Doge holds the authority to appoint new Dons. These appointments must be made with utmost scrutiny, granted only to players with a proven track record of development, loyalty, and leadership within the Republic. The Council may veto such appointments with a ⅔ majority The Doge may pass Donship of a Hold from one player to another in extreme cases, such as the unusually prolonged inactivity of a Don, or a Don who has declared themselves openly hostile to Satriale and her allies. The Doge maintains the right to create and appoint public offices, and delegate such offices amongst the Dons or loyal Citizens as the Doge sees fit. Should an appointee fail to meet the expectations of their appointed office, the Doge may remove them from that office. Such a change of office does not constitute removal of the Don’s landed titles (such as their Hold). The Doge may appoint a non-Don citizen to an office if the appointment is agreed upon by a simple majority of the Council of Dons. Article IV - The Citizenry Citizenship of a player shall be granted by a simple majority of the Council, or, in cases where a vote fails to reach a plenum of Dons within 24 hours, by the executive authority of the Doge. The Doge and Dons shall be considered part of the Citizenry. Citizens maintain the right to speak openly and plainly of their opinion on Hold and State matters, to petition their leaders, and to assemble peacefully; however, only the Doge or Dons may directly influence, alter, and vote upon policy. Citizens maintain authority over lands they have been granted, and may administer their properties as they see fit, so long as doing so does not conflict with Hold/State ordinances. Citizens are permitted to possess citizenship in other nations on a case-by-case basis, so long as their other citizenship(s) are not deemed a credible threat to national security. The Doge reserves the right to revoke the citizenship of any player, excluding the Dons. The Dons may also exercise this right with a ⅔ majority. Citizens represent Satriale to the world and will thus uphold its reputation with the utmost civility, at home and abroad, in-game and out. Citizenship shall consist of two tiers: Compagno: The base tier of citizenship. The Compagni shall enjoy the right to own land, maintain farms, and freely access resources that are made available to the citizenry such as public farms. Upon gaining citizenship, the player will be gifted a full set of iron armor and tools by the State. Uomo D’Onore: A higher tier of citizenship reserved for Compagni with a proven track record of loyalty, contribution, and activity in the State. The Uomini D’Onore shall enjoy access to a subset of factories granted by the Doge that will aid in their efforts to grow the nation. They are also eligible to hold State offices and will be gifted a full set of diamond armor. Article V - Sindacos Cities/Townships/etc may carry out free elections for a Sindaco – or Mayor. In the case of Cities/Townships/etc that a Don personally rules over, they are considered to be the Mayor of these jurisdictions by default, but they are welcome to allow elections for Mayoral candidates to manage their Cities as well. A Sindaco’s responsibility is to ensure smooth operation of their jurisdiction, to meet the needs of their citizens to the best of their ability, and to act as a liaison between citizens and the Don of their Hold, if they exist within a Hold. Sindacos are Community Leaders – ultimately, the purpose of their role is to lead their community in decision making, organizing events, and prioritizing tasks. Sindacos cannot pass legislation in their own right, but they may suggest new ordinances to their Dons or the Doge that they think would benefit the needs of the citizens within their jurisdiction. The term length for Sindacos will begin at 1 month, after which an election must be held with a simple majority vote for the new Sindaco. Any jurisdiction may vote to change the term length for Sindacos of their community at any time with a ⅔ majority. Article VI - State Territory All lands not currently administered as Holds shall be considered State Territory. The Doge may grant State Territory to individuals or communities who establish meaningful development, thereby creating new Holds or expanding existing ones. Should a Hold be abandoned, it shall be reverted to State Territory by the Doge. A Hold shall be considered abandoned if its Don remains inactive without contact for an unusually long period of time without appointing an active heir to their Donship. Article VII - Law and Order State law shall apply to all citizens within the Republic. Local laws may exist within Holds, provided they do not contradict national law. Enforcement of law within Holds is the responsibility of the Don. State law enforcement is the responsibility of the Doge. Any Don or holder of an appointed State office may propose and sponsor a Bill, but each Bill may only be voted into Law by the Dons. In cases where the Doge and Dons agree that a measure is better suited to a vote amongst the Citizenry, a Bill may be proposed as a State referendum in which all citizens are eligible to cast a vote. The Dons and other officials will respect the sanctity of the voting Floor. A Bill shall be written with care and with clear intent before being brought to the Floor. Informal discussion of policy should be conducted among sponsors and peers before formally presenting it as a Bill. No Bill shall be passed into Law that directly contradicts this Constitution or its amendments. Article VIII - Amendments Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed by the Doge or by any Don. Amendments shall pass with a ⅔ approval of the Council.
