Constitution of Satriale

Article I – The State: The State shall be known as The Serene Republic of Satriale. It shall be divided between state-administered territory and Don-administered Holds.

Article II – The Dons: Each recognized Don governs a Hold consisting of their lands. They collectively form the Council of Dons, advising the Doge and voting on state matters.

Article III – The Doge: The Doge is the Head of State and oversees diplomacy, governance, and national security. Appointments and removals are governed by the constitution.

Article IV – Citizenry: Citizens may own land, participate in elections, and maintain properties. Citizenship tiers: Compagno and Uomo D’Onore.

Article V – Sindacos: Cities may elect Sindacos (Mayors) to manage local communities and liaise with Dons.

Article VI – State Territory: Unadministered lands are State Territory. Doge may grant lands to individuals or communities to form new Holds.

Article VII – Law and Order: State law applies to all citizens. Local laws must not contradict national law. Bills may be proposed by Dons or the Doge.

Article VIII – Amendments: Amendments may be proposed by the Doge or any Don and require ⅔ approval of the Council.
