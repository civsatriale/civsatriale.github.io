Constitution of Satriale

The Constitution of Satriale lays out the fundamental laws and principles that uphold the republic. It enshrines rights for all citizens: freedom of speech, religion, and assembly.

It defines the structure of government, the separation of powers, and the processes for amendments and reforms. Its goal is to protect the dignity and liberty of every individual.
