History of Satriale

The nation was founded by the historically recognized Dons, establishing the Serene Republic with structured governance and laws.

Major milestones include the establishment of State Territory, creation of Holds, and the election of Sindacos in various communities.

The nation continues to grow with citizens contributing infrastructure, trade, and diplomacy.
