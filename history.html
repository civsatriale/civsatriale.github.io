History of Satriale

Founded centuries ago in a small valley, Satriale has grown into a stable republic rooted in tradition. The founding families forged its early government based on mutual respect, and their values passed down through generations.

Over the decades, Satriale endured hardship and peace — wars that tested its resolve, and treaties that shaped its borders. Its citizens remain deeply connected to their past, honoring the elders and their legacy.
