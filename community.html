Community

Citizens play an active role in Satriale. Compagni and Uomo D’Onore contribute to infrastructure, farming, and defense.

Participate in elections for Sindacos, suggest ordinances, or collaborate on public projects. Community engagement ensures the prosperity and reputation of the Republic.

Forums, Discord channels, and in-game meetings serve as hubs for discussion, planning, and celebration of accomplishments.
